## About

I'm a frontend and web developer from Karachi who likes building products that are fast, responsive, and easy to use. Most of my work sits around **React, Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS**, with a solid backend foundation in **Node.js, Laravel, Prisma, PostgreSQL, MySQL, and MongoDB**, plus **WordPress** for CMS-driven sites.

I've shipped storefront-style marketing sites, filterable product catalogs, admin dashboards, billing and inventory systems, and Stripe payment flows. I also spent over a year teaching web development at Aptech, where I mentored 100+ students and learned that explaining a concept is the fastest way to master it.

I'm currently going deeper into **Shopify theme development and Liquid**, and I'm open to frontend, eCommerce, and junior Shopify roles in Karachi.

> Currently: learning Shopify Liquid, building eCommerce interfaces, and taking on work that values clean UI and thoughtful details.
