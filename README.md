## About

I'm a full-stack engineer from Karachi. I started with frontend and eCommerce, then followed the hard parts into APIs, data models, and services in **Rust** and **Python**.

For the last year I've taught web development at **Aptech** — 100+ students across frontend, backend and capstone work. Teaching forced me to make the basics precise: state, data flow, auth, testing, and the difference between a demo and something that holds up.

My data work comes from mechanical-engineering coursework in energy and condition monitoring. I like problems where the model has to be evaluated honestly — including the cases where the answer is “this does not generalise.”

**Currently:** building [MechSense](https://github.com/Shahbaz2104/mechsense), an industrial machine-health monitor, and looking for a full-time full-stack or AI/ML engineering role.
